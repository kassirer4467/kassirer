Moshe Kassirer

About Moshe Kassirer

עברית

I begin painting something, but at a certain point the painting starts making choices of its own. That process is what interests and guides me.
I love the feeling of losing control, of not being the one making the decisions. My role is to create something alive, something that can say new things.
I want my paintings to have an intelligence of their own, not to be mere servants of my meaning.

C.V.

  • 1992: Studied painting and art at the Avni Institute, Tel Aviv.
  • 1993: Participated in a workshop with the artist Naftali Golomb, Tel Aviv.
  • 1994: Studied filmmaking at the Camera Obscura School of Art, Tel Aviv.
  • 1995: Won the America-Israel Cultural Foundation scholarship.
  • 1997: Designer in the film and advertising industry.
  • 1999: Designer in the Web industry.
  • 2005: Participated in a workshop with the artist Robert Bosler.
  • 2006: Participated in a workshop with the artist Don Jusko.
  • 2008: Painting became my main profession.
